# SCVAE-XC: making SCVAE work across cultures — plan

*24 October 2026. Started at the user's request: Stage 1 runs straight away; later stages may be edited after
Stage 1's result. Each stage's prediction is written before its runs and stays as written.*

## Goal and honest target

Extend SCVAE-Net (VAE encoder with SE blocks, supervised contrastive loss, uncertainty-weighted MMD) from
within-culture to cross-culture emotion recognition, and close most of the gap between fair cross-culture and
fair within-culture accuracy.

| Within-culture number | What it is | Reachable fairly across cultures? |
|---|---|---|
| 93.1% (SCVAE paper) | Within Chinese, checkpoint chosen on test labels, test people saw the training films | No: inflated twice |
| 74–80% (SCVAE strict 74.3, v3 same-culture 80.0) | Fair, within one culture | **The honest bar** |
| ~65% | Current fair cross-culture best (NormFusion v3) | Where we are |
| 53.3% | SCVAE-fixed, fair, cross-culture (3-seed ensemble) | SCVAE's starting point |

Success for the full model: about 70–74% fair, i.e. most of the cross- vs within-culture gap closed. Every table
has a fair column (the only accuracy) and, where shown, a separately labelled test-label-selected column.

## Why SCVAE fails across cultures (our own evidence)

1. **Errors are person × film.** A person's whole clip is consistently right or wrong (averaging over a clip adds
   +0.6), but the same film is right for some people and wrong for others (most films "mixed").
2. **Blind alignment.** Class-blind MMD pulls the whole target culture onto the source mixture, regardless of
   emotion.
3. **Film learning.** Accuracy falls on unseen films; part of the signal is film recognition (E1, E2, Part 2).
4. **One culture, EEG only.** v3 gets ~70% from two training cultures vs ~65% from one; eye features help.
   SCVAE uses neither.

## Components (each aimed at one failure)

① **Stimulus-locked contrastive pretraining (SLC) — main novelty.** Within a culture, every person's windows are
time-aligned (identical window counts per film for all recordings: 15/15, 18/18, 21/21 films checked). Windows of
different people at the same moment of the same film are positive pairs; other moments are negatives. Pretrain
the SCVAE encoder with this InfoNCE loss on the unlabelled windows of the source and the **target** culture (no
emotion labels: fair, transductive like MMD and self-training). Learns what a culture's brains share at the same
moment while removing individual differences. CLISA (Shen et al.) did this within Chinese people; across cultures
it is new. Targets failure 1.

② **Split latent: emotion vs nuisance.** Latent = emotion part + nuisance part; adversarial heads (gradient
reversal) stop the emotion part from predicting the film and the culture; the decoder reconstructs from both.
Targets failure 3 and ties the method to the film-confound finding.

③ **Emotion-aware alignment.** Class-conditional alignment with confident pseudo-labels and prototypes, plus a
per-person equal-emotion constraint (balanced decoding already gave +0.8 to +1.1 on v3). Targets failure 2.

④ **Two training cultures + eye branch + per-recording normalisation.** Train on two cultures, test on the third,
with a culture-invariance penalty; add a 33-feature eye branch. Targets failure 4 (worth ~+5 in v3).

⑤ **Per-person test-time adaptation.** A few steps of class-balanced entropy minimisation (SHOT / TENT style) on
each new person, without labels.

## Expected effect (fair accuracy, cross-culture average)

| Setup | Expected |
|---|---:|
| SCVAE-fixed (now) | 53 |
| + ④ two cultures + eye + normalisation | 62–66 |
| + ③ + ⑤ | 65–69 |
| + ① + ② | **68–74, if ① works** |

## Stages

### Stage 1 — SLC alone on top of SCVAE-fixed (go / no-go)

- **Arms:** SCVAE-fixed (existing runs: the configuration chosen per test culture from the other two cultures'
  transfers, seeds 12/34/56, `results/scvae_improve/grid`) vs the same configuration and seeds with the encoder
  initialised from SLC pretraining (`--init-encoder`, run suffix `__slc`). Everything else identical.
- **SLC pretraining** (`src/experiments/pretrain_slc.py`), once per (source, target, seed): the SCVAE encoder
  (latent 256) + projection head (256 → 128); per-recording normalisation as in fine-tuning; each batch from one
  culture (alternating source / target), 128 (film, moment) keys, 2 different recordings per key, moment jitter
  ±1 window, Gaussian input noise 0.1; NT-Xent, temperature 0.1; Adam 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4, 3000 steps. All
  fixed in advance; no emotion label is read.
- **Linear-probe diagnostic** (cheap, same script): logistic regression on source embeddings (source labels),
  scored on the target, for SLC vs a random-initialised encoder vs raw features.
- **Prediction:** SCVAE-fixed + SLC 3-seed ensemble 56% (vs 53.3%); linear probe SLC > raw features by 2 points.
- **Go:** SLC ensemble ≥ +2 points over SCVAE-fixed on average and higher in ≥ 4 of 6 directions. No-go: stop ①,
  keep the negative result, move to ④ + ③ + ⑤.

### Stage 2 — ④, then ③, then ⑤, one at a time

Each step added to the best model so far, scored on the 6 one-to-one directions and the 3 two-cultures → third
settings, 3 seeds; a prediction and a go criterion written before each step.

### Stage 3 — ② and the full model

Full SCVAE-XC with 3 seeds, all 6 directions + 3 two-culture settings, an ablation table (remove one component at
a time), the unseen-film test (Part 2 folds), and the separately labelled test-label-selected column.

## Paper story

Cross-cultural EEG emotion recognition: published numbers are inflated (test-label selection, film
memorisation); SCVAE-XC — stimulus-locked contrastive learning on the new culture plus a film-invariant latent —
closes the gap honestly.

## Stage 1 result (24 October 2026, cluster job 6800)

**NO-GO.** SCVAE-fixed + SLC 52.9% vs SCVAE-fixed 53.3% (−0.4 points; higher in 2 of 6 directions; no direction
significant). Linear probe: SLC encoder 45.9% vs raw features 53.1% (random encoder 47.2%). The SLC loss falls
from ~5.7 to ~2.5, so the encoder does learn which moment of which film a window comes from, but what people
share at the same moment is film content, not transferable emotion: further evidence for the film confound.
Summary: `results/tables/scvae_xc_stage1_summary.md`. Next, per the plan: Stage 2 (④, then ③, then ⑤).

## Stage 2, step ④ — design and prediction (written before any run, 24 October 2026)

New trainer `src/experiments/train_scvae_xc.py`: SCVAE-fixed's training (losses and weights, cosine schedule,
weight decay 1e-4, target shuffle, input noise and learning rate as chosen per test culture, 100 epochs,
epoch chosen on held-out source people, BatchNorm re-estimated on the source, per-recording normalisation, one
model per test person) with two options:

- `--modality eeg_eye`: a 33-feature eye branch (Linear 33 → 64, ReLU) joined with the EEG encoder's features
  before the latent; the decoder also reconstructs the eye features.
- Two training cultures (`--sources A B`): k held-out validation people from each (3 Chinese, 2 German / French);
  an extra MMD (weight 0.1) between the two source cultures' latents in each batch (culture invariance).

Arms (3 seeds each, 3-seed ensemble scored): `eeg_1src` (6 directions; re-implementation check, expected to
match SCVAE-fixed 53.3 within ±1.5), `eye_1src` (6 directions), `eeg_2src` and `eye_2src` (3 two-cultures →
third settings).

**Prediction:** eeg_1src 53; eye_1src 57; eeg_2src 57; eye_2src 61 (two-culture average; the reference for the
same targets is SCVAE-fixed's mean of the two one-culture directions into each target, 53.3).

**Go (keep ④ for the next step):** eye_2src ≥ +5 points over that reference and higher in all 3 targets, or
eye_1src ≥ +2 points over SCVAE-fixed and higher in ≥ 4 of 6 directions.

## Stage 2, step ④ result (cluster job 6807)

**GO.** eye_1src 60.5% vs SCVAE-fixed 53.3% (+7.2, higher in 5 of 6 directions); eye_2src 67.3% vs reference
53.3% (+14.0, higher in 3 of 3, p ≤ 0.009 in each target). eeg_1src 53.9% reproduces SCVAE-fixed (re-implementation
check passed); eeg_2src 54.9%. Both eye arms beat their predictions (57 and 61). Context: NormFusion v3 fair in the
same two-culture settings is 69.8%, so with ④ SCVAE-XC catches up with v3 but does not yet beat it; ③ and ⑤ must
supply the novelty. Summary: `results/tables/scvae_xc_stage2_summary.md`. Next: step ③ on top of eye_2src.

## Stage 2, step ③ — design and prediction (written before any run, 28 October 2026)

Built on the step ④ model (eye branch; one or two training cultures), same trainer, recipe, learning rates, input
noise and seeds as the step ④ runs. New option `--align`:

- `cmmd` — **class-conditional MMD.** From epoch 11 (10 warm-up epochs of the step ④ recipe), each target window
  gets a pseudo-label from the classifier (detached); windows with confidence ≥ 0.9 are kept. For each emotion with
  ≥ 4 source and ≥ 4 kept target windows in the batch, MMD between the source latents of that emotion and the
  target latents pseudo-labelled with it; the mean over those emotions, weight 0.1 (as the global MMD, which stays).
- `ca` — `cmmd` plus a **per-person equal-emotion term**, from epoch 11: weight 1.0 × (log 3 − entropy of the
  batch-mean target prediction). Every target batch is one test person, and every SEED-family recording has equal
  clips per emotion by design (a known property of the protocol, not read from any label); it stops the
  pseudo-labels collapsing onto one emotion.

All values fixed here; nothing is tuned on any target culture. Epoch selection is unchanged (held-out source
people). Target labels are read only at the final evaluation.

Arms (3 seeds, 3-seed ensemble scored, 36 runs): `eye_1src_ca` (6 directions), `eye_2src_ca` and
`eye_2src_cmmd` (3 two-cultures → third settings). Reference: the step ④ arms `eye_1src` (60.5) and
`eye_2src` (67.3).

**Prediction:** eye_2src_cmmd 68; eye_2src_ca 69; eye_1src_ca 62.

**Go (keep ③ for step ⑤):** eye_2src_ca ≥ +1.5 points over eye_2src and higher in ≥ 2 of 3 targets, or
eye_1src_ca ≥ +1.5 points over eye_1src and higher in ≥ 4 of 6 directions. Milestone, not a go criterion:
eye_2src_ca above NormFusion v3 fair in the same settings (69.8).

## Stage 2, step ③ result (28 October 2026, cluster job 6961)

**NO-GO.** eye_2src_ca 67.7% vs eye_2src 67.3% (+0.5, higher in 2 of 3, no target significant); eye_2src_cmmd
67.4%; eye_1src_ca 59.6% vs eye_1src 60.5% (−0.9, higher in 1 of 6). All below prediction (69 / 68 / 62).
Summary: `results/tables/scvae_xc_step3_summary.md`.

Exploratory (decided after the results; uses no target label): the epoch is chosen on held-out source people, and
many runs choose an epoch inside the 10-epoch warm-up, where step ③ is not yet active (41 of 84 two-culture
runs). Restricted to runs that chose an epoch after the warm-up, per-run change vs step ④ is −1.0 (eye_1src_ca,
125 runs), −1.4 (eye_2src_ca, 49), +0.1 (eye_2src_cmmd, 49); none significant. So the null result is not due to
dilution: confident pseudo-labels across cultures are not accurate enough to align emotion by emotion, and the
balance term slightly hurts. A rerun restricted to post-warm-up epochs is not worth running.

## Stage 2, step ⑤ — design and prediction (written before any run, 28 October 2026)

Built on the step ④ model (step ③ dropped): same trainer, recipe, learning rates, input noise, seeds and epoch
selection. After the final model is chosen and its BatchNorm re-estimated on the source, new option `--tta`
adapts it to the one test person, without labels:

- Parameters updated: the encoder (EEG convolutions, attention, eye branch, `fc_mu`); the classifier is frozen
  (SHOT). The model stays in eval mode (no dropout; BatchNorm keeps the source statistics).
- `im` — information maximisation (SHOT): mean prediction entropy + (log 3 − entropy of the batch-mean
  prediction), equal weights. `ent` — prediction entropy only (TENT-style objective).
- 2 passes over the person's shuffled windows, batch 128, Adam, learning rate 1e-4, no weight decay. Fixed here;
  nothing is tuned on any target culture.

Arms (3 seeds, 3-seed ensemble, 36 runs): `eye_1src_tta_im` (6 directions), `eye_2src_tta_im` and
`eye_2src_tta_ent` (3 settings). Reference: step ④ `eye_1src` 60.5, `eye_2src` 67.3.

**Prediction:** eye_2src_tta_im 68; eye_2src_tta_ent 67; eye_1src_tta_im 61. (Step ③ showed cross-culture
confident predictions are unreliable, so the expected gain is small.)

**Go (keep ⑤):** eye_2src_tta_im ≥ +1.5 over eye_2src and higher in ≥ 2 of 3 targets, or eye_1src_tta_im ≥ +1.5
over eye_1src and higher in ≥ 4 of 6.

## Test-label-selected column for SCVAE-XC (paper protocol; never reported as accuracy)

The step ④ arms `eye_1src` and `eye_2src` are re-run with the test person's predictions saved after every epoch
(same scored copy as the source-validation check, BatchNorm re-estimated on the source; predicted from the latent
mean, so no random draw is added and training is unchanged). Re-run check: the strict 3-seed ensemble must match
the step ④ numbers within ±1 point. Then, on the 3-seed ensemble per epoch, as in the v3 paper-protocol study:

- **one setting for all:** the single epoch that maximises mean test accuracy over the direction's test people;
- **per person:** the best epoch for each test person.

Reported only in a separately labelled "test-label-selected" column next to the strict number.
**Prediction:** eye_2src: strict 67, one setting 70, per person 76; eye_1src: strict 60, one setting 63, per person 70.

## Stage 2, step ⑤ and test-label-selected column — results (28 October 2026, cluster job 6972)

**Step ⑤: NO-GO.** eye_2src_tta_im 67.7% vs eye_2src 67.3% (+0.5, higher in 2 of 3; only German + French →
Chinese significant, +1.5, p = 0.02); eye_2src_tta_ent 67.2%; eye_1src_tta_im 60.7% vs 60.5% (+0.2, 4 of 6).
Stage 2 ends with ④ only: SCVAE-XC fair 67.3% (two cultures) vs NormFusion v3 69.8%.

**Test-label-selected column (not accuracy).** Re-run strict reproduces step ④ exactly (60.5 / 67.3). Choosing the
epoch by test labels: one epoch for all 63.0 / 68.4, best epoch per person 65.9 / 72.3 (one / two cultures):
inflation +5.0 to +5.4 points, below the prediction (70 / 76) and far below original SCVAE's +20 — the regularised
model with source-person epoch selection leaves little to cherry-pick.
Summary: `results/tables/scvae_xc_step5_summary.md`.

Exploratory (28 October, saved probabilities, no target label used for decisions): clip voting per recording adds
+1.0 (v3 metric 68.3 vs 67.3); causal smoothing over 5–40 past windows adds nothing. Voting over the same film's
three sessions adds about +10 (77.5) — errors are largely recording-level (day-to-day) variation, not a stable
person × film property.

## Revised direction — film diversity (29 October 2026)

*Replaces Stage 3 as planned. Each step below gets its own prediction and go rule, written before it runs.*

**Why.** Every model plateaus at ~60–75% fair. Diagnostics (28–29 October): a person-specific model trained on the
person's own clips does no better on new clips (57–69%); the films-per-emotion learning curve rises from 50.1 (1 film)
to 61.5 (8 films) and, at the same window budget, 8 films beat 2 by +7.2
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-film-learning-curve-design.md`). The bottleneck is distinct films per emotion,
not people, cultures or the architecture; step ④'s +14 is largely "twice the films". The eye check
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-eye-confound-check-design.md`) found the pupil carries the whole eye gain
(mostly emotional vs neutral, i.e. arousal) while the 21 movement features carry film content.

**Steps on top of eye_2src (67.3), cheapest first:**

- **D1 — eeg+pupil input.** Replace the 33 eye features with the 12 pupil features (drop movement). Expected: equal
  3-class, better positive vs negative, less film-specific. One cluster run (9 lines × 3 seeds).
- **D2 — film-balanced sampling.** Source batches drawn so every film contributes equally (films differ in length;
  the loader is now uniform over windows). Directly applies "diversity over quantity".
- **D3 — cross-film mixup within an emotion.** Mix latents (or inputs) of two different films of the same emotion,
  synthesising "new films" in feature space; label unchanged.
- **D4 — film-adversarial head (component ②).** Gradient reversal on a head that predicts the film from the latent,
  so the latent cannot encode film identity.
- **D5 — more films (needs data).** Add SEED-IV (72 clips) and SEED-V (45 clips) as extra source films once
  requested from BCMI; DE features share our format; their eye features differ (use pupil only if it maps, else EEG
  plus the shared subset). Expected, from the learning curve's flattening slope: +3 to +6.

**Honest target:** D1–D4 together +1 to +4 (to ~70); D5 is the only lever with a clear path to the mid-70s. Every
table keeps the fair column as the only accuracy, and the test-label-selected column labelled separately.

## D1 — eeg+pupil input: design and prediction (30 October 2026, written before running)

`--modality eeg_pupil`: the eye branch takes eye columns 0–11 (pupil, inferred) instead of all 33; everything else
as step ④ (same 27 lines: 6 one-culture directions and 3 two-culture targets × seeds 12/34/56, learning rate 0.0025,
no input noise). Arms `pupil_1src`, `pupil_2src`, compared with `eye_1src` (60.5) and `eye_2src` (67.3), strict
3-seed ensembles, Wilcoxon over people. Secondary: positive vs negative accuracy on positive and negative windows
only, deciding between the two classes by the ensemble's probabilities (no retraining).

**Prediction:** pupil_2src 67.5, pupil_1src 61.0; positive vs negative +1 over the eye arms.

**Decision rule:** **ADOPT** (pupil becomes the input for D2–D4) if pupil_2src ≥ eye_2src − 0.5 **and**
pupil_1src ≥ eye_1src − 1.0. Otherwise **KEEP** all 33 eye features. Positive vs negative is reported but does not
decide. Summary: `src/experiments/summarize_scvae_xc_d1.py` → `results/tables/scvae_xc_d1_summary.md`.

## D1 — result (30 October 2026, cluster job 7066)

**D1: KEEP all 33 eye features** (pre-registered rule). pupil_2src 64.4% vs eye_2src 67.3% (−2.9, lower in all 3
targets, none significant alone); pupil_1src 60.1% vs eye_1src 60.5% (−0.3; Chinese → German/French −3.5/−4.6,
German → Chinese +4.4, p = 0.02). Positive vs negative: one culture 74.5 vs 69.9 (+4.6, higher in 6 of 6), two
cultures 77.3 vs 76.3 (+1.0). Prediction (67.5 / 61.0) wrong for two cultures.

Reading: unlike logistic regression (eye check, where movement added nothing), SCVAE-XC with two training cultures
uses the movement features for the neutral class — it loses 3-class accuracy without them but separates positive
from negative better. Whether that movement gain is film content or genuine gaze behaviour is not settled by this
run. D2–D4 continue on eye_2src (all 33 features). Summary: `results/tables/scvae_xc_d1_summary.md`.

## Self-training (v3's rule) on SCVAE-XC — design and prediction (5 November 2026, written before running)

**Why:** in NormFusion v3, removing self-training costs 2.0 points with two cultures (69.8 → 67.8), the gap
between v3 and SCVAE-XC. Unlike step ⑤ (entropy/IM test-time adaptation), self-training adds pseudo-labelled
target windows to the training data with equal numbers per emotion.

`--self-train`: after training and source-person epoch selection (unchanged), up to 3 rounds. In each round, the
test person's windows predicted with confidence ≥ 0.9 are taken, the top-k per predicted emotion with k = the
smallest of the three pools; the round stops if k < 10 (v3's ST_ROUNDS / ST_THRESHOLD / ST_MIN). They are added to
the source training set with their predicted labels (excluded from the between-culture MMD), and the selected model
is fine-tuned for 10 epochs with all step ④ losses at learning rate 0.1 × the base rate. There is no further epoch
selection: the last epoch is kept, then BatchNorm is re-estimated on the source as before. No target label is read
before the final scoring. Arms `eye_2src_st`, `eye_1src_st`: the same 27 lines as step ④.

**Prediction:** eye_2src_st 68.8 (+1.5), eye_1src_st 61.5 (+1.0).

**Decision rule:** **GO** if eye_2src_st − eye_2src ≥ +1.0 averaged over the 3 targets **and** it is higher in ≥ 2 of 3
targets. The one-culture rows are reported without a decision; the v3 comparison (69.8) is context only.
Summary: `src/experiments/summarize_scvae_xc_st.py` → `results/tables/scvae_xc_st_summary.md`.

## Self-training — result (5 November 2026, cluster job 7422)

**NO-GO** (pre-registered). eye_2src_st 66.7% vs eye_2src 67.3% (−0.6; higher in 1 of 3; Chinese + French →
German −1.8, p = 0.02); eye_1src_st 60.6% vs 60.5% (+0.1, 4 of 6). All 252 runs completed and used all 3 rounds,
so too few confident windows is not the cause. Prediction (68.8 / 61.5) wrong.

Reading: v3's +2.0 does not transfer. v3 pseudo-labels the whole target culture with a bagged committee, whose
confident guesses are more reliable; SCVAE-XC pseudo-labels one person with one model that is already aligned to
that person by MMD, so the pseudo-labels add little new information and some confirmed errors. SCVAE-XC remains
at 67.3% (two cultures); v3 (69.8%) remains the best fair model.
Summary: `results/tables/scvae_xc_st_summary.md`.
